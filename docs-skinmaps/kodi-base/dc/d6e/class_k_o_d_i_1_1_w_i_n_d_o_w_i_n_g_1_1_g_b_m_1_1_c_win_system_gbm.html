◆ SetVideoOutput()

bool CWinSystemGbm::SetVideoOutput ( const VideoPicture * videoPicture)
overridevirtual

Signal the role of the output surface: video playback or idle GUI.

Called by video renderers at Configure (with videoPicture) and at UnInit (nullptr). On platforms with a flip-flop plane-role model (single-plane GBM), implementations adopt the output plane as the video plane while a video is playing and revert it to the gui plane on disable. Returns true if the role transition was applied.
